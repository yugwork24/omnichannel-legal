Privacy Policy

How OMNICHANNEL collects, uses, and protects information when a business connects its Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp, and other channels into one shared inbox.

Effective 10 Sept 2026 Version 1.0 Operator Raid Layer Webhost Pvt Ltd Contact v91863126@gmail.com

01 Scope & who we are

This policy covers OMNICHANNEL, a customer-messaging platform operated by Raid Layer Webhost Pvt Ltd ("we", "us", "our") that lets a business team send and receive messages across Facebook Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp, email, and web chat from a single shared inbox.

This policy applies to the businesses that use OMNICHANNEL ("customers") and, through them, to the end users who message a customer's connected channels ("contacts"). It does not cover third-party platforms — Meta, Google, and others each publish their own privacy terms for the accounts you hold with them directly.

02 Information we collect

From a customer account

  • Account and billing details: name, email, company name, plan and billing status.
  • Team data: agent names, roles, and login activity within the workspace.
  • Channel credentials: OAuth tokens and identifiers issued by Meta and other providers when a customer connects a Page, Instagram account, WhatsApp Business Account, or mailbox. Tokens are stored encrypted at rest and are never shown in plain text after entry.

From a contact (an end user messaging a connected channel)

  • Identifiers the channel provides: a Page-scoped or Instagram-scoped user ID, or a phone number for WhatsApp.
  • Public profile fields the channel makes available: display name and profile photo.
  • The content of messages sent through a connected channel — text, attachments, reactions, and delivery/read status — for as long as needed to display and manage the conversation.

03 Connected messaging channels

When a customer connects a channel, OMNICHANNEL requests only the permissions that channel to function as a shared inbox:

  • Facebook Messenger — pages_messaging, to send and receive messages on behalf of a Page the customer administers.
  • Instagram — instagram_business_basic and instagram_business_manage_messages, to send and receive direct messages on behalf of an Instagram professional account the customer administers.
  • WhatsApp — whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management, to send and receive messages through a WhatsApp Business Account the customer administers.

We do not request access to a customer's or contact's broader social profile, contacts list, or content unrelated to the messaging conversation itself.

04 How we use information

  • To display conversations in the customer's shared inbox and route them to the right team or agent.
  • To send messages the customer's team composes back to a contact, on the channel the contact originally used.
  • To power optional features the customer enables, such as AI-assisted reply drafting or automation rules, using message content already visible to the customer's team.
  • To maintain the security, availability, and integrity of the service, including detecting abuse of connected channel credentials.

We do not sell information we hold on behalf of customers, and we do not use contact message content to build advertising profiles.

05 Sharing & disclosure

Information is shared only where necessary to operate the service:

  • With Meta and other channel providers, strictly to transmit and receive messages through the channel a customer has connected.
  • With infrastructure sub-processors (hosting, database, and object storage providers) who process data solely to run OMNICHANNEL on our behalf.
  • Where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of OMNICHANNEL, our customers, or the public.

06 Retention

Conversation and contact data is retained for as long as a customer's account remains active, so their team keeps a working history of every conversation. Channel access tokens are retained only until a customer disconnects the channel or revokes access from the provider's own settings.

07 Data deletion

A customer can disconnect a channel at any time from their Integrations settings, which revokes and deletes the stored access token immediately.

Requesting deletion

To request deletion of a customer's account data, or of a specific contact's message history, email v91863126@gmail.com with the workspace name and, for a contact-specific request, the channel and conversation involved. We confirm and complete deletion requests within 30 days.

If a contact reaches us through Facebook, Instagram, or WhatsApp and asks that platform to delete their data, we honor the resulting deletion callback from that platform in the same way as a direct request.

08 Security

  • Channel access tokens are encrypted at rest using envelope encryption; no plaintext token is logged or displayed after initial entry.
  • All traffic to and from OMNICHANNEL is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Access to customer data within our own systems is limited to what a role needs to operate or support the service.
